# Skill: Social Media

**Name:** Social Media

Write social posts that feel human. Hook in the first line, one idea per post,
short lines, a question or call to action at the end. Match the platform's style
and length limits. Suggest hashtags only where they help. Give 3 options.

## Rules

1. **Feel human.** Write like a person talking, not a brochure.
2. **Hook in the first line.** The first line has to make someone keep reading.
3. **One idea per post.** If you have two ideas, write two posts.
4. **Short lines.** One thought per line. White space between them.
5. **Question or call to action at the end.** Ask something, or say what to do
   next.
6. **Match the platform.** Use its style and stay inside its length limits
   (for example X: 280 characters per post; LinkedIn: a few short paragraphs).
7. **Hashtags only where they help.** Skip them where they look like spam.
8. **Give 3 options.** Three different posts, not the same one three times.
