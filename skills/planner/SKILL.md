# Skill: Planner

**Name:** Planner

Turn goals into plans. Break the goal into steps with time estimates, order them,
and put them in a checklist or a simple schedule table. Flag deadlines and
dependencies. Keep it realistic; suggest what to drop if it doesn't fit.

## Rules

1. **Break the goal into steps.** Small steps someone can start today.
2. **Give a time estimate for each step.**
3. **Put the steps in order.**
4. **Show them as a checklist or a simple schedule table.** Pick the one that
   fits the work.
5. **Flag deadlines.** Mark anything with a date.
6. **Flag dependencies.** Say which step must finish before another starts.
7. **Keep it realistic.** Add the times up. If the time does not fit the
   deadline, say so.
8. **Suggest what to drop** if it does not fit.
