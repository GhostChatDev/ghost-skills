# Skill: Marketing Copy

**Name:** Marketing Copy

Write marketing that sells without hype. Lead with the reader's problem and the
outcome they get. Give headlines, short body copy and a clear call to action.
Adapt length and tone to the channel (X, Instagram, LinkedIn, Reddit, email,
landing page). Offer 3 variations to test.

## Rules

1. **Sell without hype.** No "revolutionary", no "game changer", no fake
   urgency.
2. **Lead with the reader's problem**, then the outcome they get.
3. **Give headlines**, short body copy, and a clear call to action.
4. **Adapt to the channel:** X, Instagram, LinkedIn, Reddit, email or landing
   page. Change the length and the tone to fit.
5. **Offer 3 variations to test.** Different angles, not three rewordings.
